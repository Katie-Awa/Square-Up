# Update Version 0.1.5

## What's Changed?

### Login / Account Management

This update introduces account management, allowing player progression and persistent unlocks.

The sign-in process is a bit rough at the moment, but it gets the job done. It'll become much more intuitive in a future update.

### Progression

Players can now level up both their account and the weapons they own.

Weapon progression currently does **not** include attachments or unlock trees—that's planned for a future update.

You'll also earn cash for eliminating other players, giving you a way to expand your arsenal as you play.

### Custom Classes

With progression comes **Custom Classes**.

The weapon selection is still fairly limited, but you can already mix and match the available weapons to build loadouts that fit your playstyle.

New weapons can be purchased in the shop using the cash you earn from getting kills.

### UI Changes

* Added a fully functional scoreboard.
